## 🎯 First-Time Publishers

If this is your first app publication:

1. **Start:** [PUBLICATION_GUIDE.md](./PUBLICATION_GUIDE.md)
2. **Create accounts:** Apple Developer ($99), Google Play ($25)
3. **Design:** Use [APP_ICONS_SPLASH.md](./APP_ICONS_SPLASH.md)
4. **Write:** Follow [APPSTORE_ASSETS.md](./APPSTORE_ASSETS.md)
5. **Secure:** Complete [CERTIFICATES_SIGNING.md](./CERTIFICATES_SIGNING.md)
6. **Test:** Use [TESTING_CHECKLIST.md](./TESTING_CHECKLIST.md)
7. **Submit:** Follow [BUILD_AND_RELEASE.md](./BUILD_AND_RELEASE.md)

**Total time:** 10-14 days working 2-3 hours daily
