## PRIVACY POLICY

**Last Updated:** March 29, 2026

### 1. Introduction

Habitick ("App," "we," "us," "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

### 2. Information We Collect

#### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, and password (stored securely)
- **Google Authentication:** When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email and user ID (from Google's authentication system)
- **Task Data:** Habits, tasks, frequency, categories, points, and progress tracking
- **Study Items:** Books, Quran verses, German words, or other items you track with study tasks
- **Encouragement Messages:** Any personalized messages generated by our AI system

#### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, mobile network information
- **App Usage:** Features used, frequency of usage, actions taken within the app
- **Analytics:** App crashes, performance metrics, feature engagement
- **Location Data:** We do NOT collect location data unless you explicitly grant permission and choose to use location-based features

#### 2.3 Third-Party Information

- **Google Authentication:** Email and user ID from your Google account
- **OpenAI:** When we generate AI encouragement, we send your task information to OpenAI's API (see section 5)
- **Analytics Services:** Usage patterns and crash reports

### 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

- **Provide Services:** Create and manage your account, track habits, generate AI encouragement
- **Improve App:** Analyze usage patterns to improve features and performance
- **Communications:** Send you account-related notifications and updates
- **Security:** Prevent fraud and maintain App security
- **Legal Compliance:** Comply with applicable laws and regulations
- **Support:** Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support

**We will NOT:**
- Sell your personal data to third parties
- Share your data for marketing purposes without consent
- Use your data for purposes other than those listed above (unless you opt-in)

### 4. Data Storage & Security

- **Storage Location:** Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL database) with encryption at rest
- **Encryption:** Data in transit uses SSL/TLS encryption (HTTPS)
- **Backups:** Encrypted backups maintained by Supabase
- **Access Control:** Only authorized systems can access your data
- **Google OAuth:** Authentication handled by Google's secure servers; we only receive your email and user ID

**Security Measures:**
- Row-level security policies on all database tables
- Service accounts with minimal permissions
- Regular security audits
- Immediate response to security incidents

**Limitations:** While we implement strong security measures, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

### 5. Third-Party Services

#### 5.1 Google Authentication (Google LLC)

- **Purpose:** Sign in securely using your Google account

- **Data Retention:** OpenAI may retain API logs for 30 days for debugging. See their [API Privacy](https://openai.com/policies/api-data-usage-policies)
- **Opt-Out:** You can choose not to use the AI encouragement feature

#### 5.3 Analytics (if applicable)

- **Service:** [Firebase Analytics / Sentry / Amplitude - fill in as applicable]
- **Purpose:** Track app crashes and feature usage

### 6. Data Retention

- **Account Data:** Retained as long as your account is active, plus 90 days after deletion
- **Deleted Accounts:** All personal data permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion request
- **Task History:** Retained indefinitely for your reference (you can manually delete)
- **AI Interaction Logs:** Retained for 30 days (OpenAI) and 7 days (our servers)

### 7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

#### 7.1 GDPR Rights (EU/EEA users)

- **Right to Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Right to Correction:** Update inaccurate information
- **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of your data ("Right to be Forgotten")
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Limit how we use your data
- **Right to Data Portability:** Export your data in machine-readable format
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Opt-out of optional data processing

#### 7.2 CCPA Rights (California residents)

- **Right to Know:** What personal information we collect
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of your data (with exceptions)
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Opt-out of data sales (we don't sell data)
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** We don't discriminate for exercising rights

#### 7.3 Other Regulations

- **Canada (PIPEDA):** Similar rights to GDPR
- **Other Regions:** We honor data protection laws of your jurisdiction

**To Exercise Your Rights:**

Email: [your-contact-email@habitick.com](mailto:your-contact-email@habitick.com)  
Include: Your account email, specific request, and any supporting documents

**Response Time:** 30 days (we may request additional time if complex)

### 8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

**If we discover we have collected data from a child under 13:**
- We will promptly delete such data
- We will not use the data for any purpose
- Please contact us immediately

## PUBLISHING TO APP STORES

### iOS App Store Requirements

Apple requires a Privacy Policy link. You must:

1. **Host it publicly** (e.g., GitHub Pages, personal website)
2. **Provide URL** in App Store Connect > Privacy Policy field
3. **Keep it updated** when you change data practices
4. **Make it accessible** from within the app (optional: add footer link)

### Google Play Requirements

Google requires:
1. **Privacy Policy link** in Play Console
2. **Clear data practices disclosure** (which types of data you collect)
3. **Valid email for privacy contact**
4. **Honesty** - if you don't collect data, say so explicitly
